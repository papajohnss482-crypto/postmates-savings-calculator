Uber One Break-Even Calculator

The membership, as shown in the app in October 2026 (checked October 2, re-checked October 12): free for 4 weeks, then $9.99 per month, $0 delivery fee on eligible orders, up to 10 percent off. This tool answers the only question that decides it: do your monthly delivery fees pass $9.99?

This compares fees only, the strictest test. Percentage discounts, which vary by order, are treated as a bonus on top, never as the reason to join.

Two traps this tool exists to prevent

  • The forgotten trial. The 4 week free trial converts to $9.99 per month automatically. If you take the trial, set the cancellation reminder the same day, not in week four.
  • Counting discounts you would not have spent. Up to 10 percent off is only a saving on orders you were placing anyway. Ordering more to justify a membership is spending dressed as saving.
