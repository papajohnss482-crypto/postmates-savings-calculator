Method and Sources

What this tool calculates, and what it refuses to

The calculator does arithmetic on numbers you type in: your subtotal, your fees, your code's terms. It runs entirely in your browser, nothing you enter is sent anywhere. What it refuses to do is guess a code for you. A tool that prints a code it cannot test would be guessing with your money.

Program terms used on this site

  • $12 Meal Deals: $12 set meals, $0 delivery fee, $0 service fee, participating restaurants, while supplies last. Captured from the Postmates app on October 2, 2026 and re-checked live and unchanged on October 12, 2026.
  • Uber One: 4 week free trial, then $9.99 per month, $0 delivery fee on eligible orders, up to 10 percent off, 5 percent back on rides. Same check dates as above. The $29 average monthly saving figure sometimes quoted for Uber One is Uber's own stated average in the app, and this site does not use it in any calculation.

Worked examples are labeled

Every table on this site that uses sample prices or sample code terms is marked as a worked example. Examples teach the math, they are not offers. Real offers are the ones marked verified in-app with a date, plus whatever your own app shows you today.

Code terms source

When this site references what a current code is worth, the terms are checked against the tracking maintained at Postmates Coupons, the source list for current Postmates codes with per code check dates and delisting records. If a code matters to your order, confirm it inside the app under Account, Promotions, Add promo code, where acceptance and the real expiry for your account are shown.

Date honesty

Check dates on this site are the dates the checks actually happened. They are never edited forward to look newer. When a program's terms change, the dated record here becomes a snapshot of October 2026, and we would rather show you an honest old date than a fake new one.

Postmates Savings Calculator is an independent tool, not affiliated with Postmates or Uber. Confirm all terms in the app before ordering.
