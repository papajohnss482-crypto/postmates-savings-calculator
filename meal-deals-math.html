The $12 Meal Deals Math

Terms checked in the Postmates app on October 2, 2026 and re-checked on October 12, 2026: set meals priced at $12, with $0 delivery fee and $0 service fee, at participating restaurants, rotating selection, while supplies last.

Why the structure matters more than the headline

A normal Postmates order is charged in layers: the food, a delivery fee, and a service fee on top. A code usually attacks only one layer, and often the smallest one. The Meal Deals structure removes two layers entirely. The meal is $12, the delivery fee is $0, and the service fee is $0. What you see is close to what you pay, before tax and tip.

Worked example

Worked example Prices below are sample prices to show the structure. Your restaurant's menu prices will differ, the fee structure is the part that carries over.

Regular order$12 Meal Deal
Meal$14.50 (entree and side at menu prices)$12.00 (set meal)
Delivery fee$3.99$0
Service fee$1.89 (about 13 percent)$0
Total before tax and tip$20.38$12.00

In this example the Meal Deal saves $8.38, and $5.88 of that saving is fees, not food. That is why a 20 percent code on the regular order, worth $2.90 here, still loses to the Meal Deal structure. Run your own restaurant's numbers in the main calculator to see your version of this table.

When a Meal Deal does not win

  • You wanted a specific dish. The deals are a rotating set selection. If the meal you want is not in the set, the comparison is against a meal you did not choose.
  • Portions differ. A set meal can be sized differently from the regular menu version. Compare what arrives, not just the price.
  • Your restaurant is not participating. The program runs at select locations, and the app marks the current order as valid while supplies last.
  • You have a strong percentage code and a big order. On multi-person orders, a percentage code on a large subtotal can pass the Meal Deals saving. The crossover logic is on the calculator page.

The honest limit of this page

Menu prices, portions, and participation change by restaurant and by week. What we verified in-app in October 2026 is the structure: $12 set meals, $0 delivery fee, $0 service fee, at participating places. Re-check the structure in your own app before relying on it, it takes ten seconds and it is the same check we do.
